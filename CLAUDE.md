# Mother Roaster — Brand Brief & Build Notes

## The brand in one sentence

Mother Roaster is the Bangkok specialty-coffee brand built around Ploenpit Rianmek ("Pa Pim"), a grandmother who opened her first wooden coffee stand in 2019 at age 70 — and who now anchors four rooms across the city where every cup is hand-brewed, never sweetened, and served above the working junkyard, riverside shophouse, ghost-gate corner, or quiet residential lane that the brand has made its own.

## Tagline (verbatim, the brand mantra)

**FOREVER / COFFEE / LOVER**

Used as a typographic anchor — all caps, slash-separated. Treated as a single phrase on the site's hero.

## Owner story (the spine)

Ploenpit Rianmek — "Pa Pim" / "Auntie Pim" — spent ~30 years brewing coffee at home. After retirement she refused to "sit around or nap, wasting oxygen" (her own words). In February 2019, at 70, she opened a two-stool wooden stand near Hua Lamphong with a British-designed ROK manual espresso maker and went viral within weeks. November 2019 she relocated to the upper floor of a working mechanic's shophouse in Talat Noi — you still walk through scrap-metal piles to reach her café. Her son co-runs the operation and contributes original art. No syrup, no sugar — "My life is already sweet, so I don't see the need to add sugar." Quotes verified across Khaosod English, Coconuts Bangkok, BK Magazine, Malay Mail/Yahoo News, The Smart Local, iDiscover Asia.

## Brand voice

Casual, warm, slightly sardonic. Thai-English mix. Anti-hype. Maternal but sharp. The voice of an opinionated aunt who knows her craft and doesn't suffer poseurs.

## Aesthetic — five words

Junkyard-to-jewelbox, wabi-grandma, Talat-Noi-vintage, slow-living, anti-aesthetic.
