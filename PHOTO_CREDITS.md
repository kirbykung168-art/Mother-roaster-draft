## Image processing

- All raw downloads were re-encoded to **WebP** (1600w + 800w) at quality 78–80 and to **AVIF** (1600w + 800w) at quality 55–60. Originals kept in `images/raw/` (gitignored).
- 34 unique source photos → 45 unique image stems on site → ~135 optimized files served.

## What we did NOT use

- Daniel Food Diary (https://danielfooddiary.com/2022/08/01/motherroaster/) — site has hotlink protection; images returned an HTML placeholder rather than the file, so they are not on the site. The article is referenced for menu price verification only.
- Direct Instagram photos — Instagram's CDN blocks server-side fetching. For a production launch, we recommend Mother Roaster grant photo licensing directly from `@motherroaster` for the strongest visual update.
